About SaveSpace

SaveSpace is a simple storage space booking website designed for people in Palawan who need a safe and accessible place to temporarily store their belongings.

The idea behind SaveSpace is to provide storage options for students, families, travellers, and small business owners who may not have enough space at home or at work.

Who SaveSpace Is For

  • Students who need temporary storage during school breaks
  • Families who are moving or renovating
  • Travellers who need short-term luggage storage
  • Small business owners who need extra inventory space

Our Goal

Our goal is to make finding temporary storage in Palawan simpler and more convenient through an easy-to-use website.
