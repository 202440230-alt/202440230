Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SaveSpace respects your privacy. This page explains what information we ask for and how we use it.

1. Information we ask for

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your contact number
  • Your reservation details, such as unit size and move-in date

2. How we use it

  1. To create and manage your account.
  2. To confirm your reservation and contact you about it.
  3. To send reminders about rent that is due.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is only seen by the SaveSpace owner and staff who handle reservations.

4. Keeping your information safe

Your password is never shown on screen. Please do not share your login with anyone.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to update or remove your information at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact

Email savespace@gmail.com or call 0995 093 9228.
